## What it is
Swiggy is an app to order **food** from restaurants and get it delivered home. The same app also offers **Instamart** (groceries and household items delivered quickly), and **Dineout** (booking a table at restaurants). Availability depends on the area.

## Words explained simply
- **Delivery address**: where the order will be brought. The app can use your phone's location, but you must confirm it.
- **Cart**: the basket of items you have chosen, before you pay.
- **Coupon / offer**: a discount code or deal. Apply only what is shown inside the app.
- **Delivery partner**: the person who brings your order.
- **Veg and non-veg marks**: a **green** dot on an item means vegetarian. A **red or brown** dot means non-vegetarian. [CHECK]
- **Swiggy One**: a paid membership that can give free delivery and other benefits. It is optional.
- **COD (Cash on Delivery)**: paying the delivery person. Not available everywhere or for every order.

## Task: Order food
1. Open Swiggy and **check the delivery address** at the top of the screen. Change it if it is not where you are.
2. Use the **search bar** to type a dish or restaurant name, or scroll through nearby restaurants. [CHECK]
3. Open a restaurant and choose items. Look at the green and red dots. Tap the button to **add** an item. Some items ask you to pick a size or extras.
4. Open the **cart**. Check the items, quantity and the **bill total** (item cost, delivery fee, taxes and charges).
5. Confirm the address again, then choose how to pay: UPI, card, or cash on delivery if shown.
6. Place the order. A tracking screen appears.

## Task: Track the order
The tracking screen shows stages: waiting for the restaurant to accept, being prepared, picked up and out for delivery. Live map tracking begins after the delivery partner picks up the food. Typical delivery takes around half an hour or more, longer in busy hours or rain.

## Task: Cancel or change an order
- Cancelling is easiest **very soon after placing** the order, within about a minute. After that, a **cancellation fee may apply**, or cancelling may not be allowed, especially once the restaurant starts preparing it. The app shows what is possible.
- Go to the order's tracking screen and open **Help** or **Help & Support** for that order to cancel or ask for a change. [CHECK]
- Do not promise a refund. Say the app shows the decision.

## Problems with a delivered order
- **Item missing, wrong, spilled or cold food:** do not throw the food or packaging. Open the order in **Orders** (past orders) and use **Help & Support** for that order. Choose the issue and add photos. Refunds, if approved, usually go back to the original payment method, or to Swiggy money. The app shows details.
- **Order not delivered but shows delivered:** report at once through Help & Support, and check with neighbours or the building security.
- **Payment deducted but order not placed:** do not pay again straight away. Check Orders. If it is not there, contact Help & Support. A failed payment is usually auto-reversed by the bank within a few working days; do not promise an exact period.

## Watch out for (money habits)
- **Charges** beyond the dish price (delivery fee, platform fee, taxes). Always read the final bill before paying.
- **Swiggy One** or a free trial that is auto-added at checkout: ask the learner to look for it and untick it unless they want it. Explain that trials may become paid later.
- Ordering from a **wrong address** (for example an old saved address).
- Cash on delivery with no exact change: keep small notes.

## Delivery tips
- Sometimes a **delivery code (OTP)** is shown for an order. Tell it to the delivery partner **at your door** only when you take the food. Never over a phone call.
- Never go outside to meet a delivery partner in an unsafe place. They can come to your gate or door.

## Swiggy-specific scams (teach these)
- A caller says "your order is stuck/refund is pending" and asks for OTP, card details, or sends a link: scam.
- A caller asks you to install a screen-sharing app "to fix your refund": scam.
- A "Swiggy customer care" number found by searching online: scam risk. Use **Help & Support** in the app.
- A WhatsApp message promising free food, cashback or a membership by tapping a link: do not tap.
- A request to pay a small "fee" or "scan a QR to receive your refund": scam. Refunds are never collected by paying.
