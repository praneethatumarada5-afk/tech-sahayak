## What it is
PhonePe is a mobile app to pay and receive money directly between bank accounts using UPI (Unified Payments Interface). No cash or card needed. It works on a smartphone with a mobile number linked to the bank account.

## Words explained simply
- **UPI**: a way to send money from your bank account to another account using your phone, like handing over cash but digitally.
- **UPI PIN**: a secret 4 or 6 digit number you set for your bank account. Like an ATM PIN. You enter it **only when money is going out of your account**.
- **UPI ID**: an address for receiving money, like name@bank, similar to a postal address.
- **QR code**: a square picture that shops display. Scanning it with PhonePe fills in the shop's details.
- **Linked bank account**: the bank account the money leaves from.
- **Transaction history**: the list of all payments you made or received. [CHECK: exact tab name]

## First-time setup (usually done once, often with family help)
1. Install PhonePe only from the official Play Store or App Store.
2. Open it and enter the mobile number that is linked to the bank account. The app verifies the number by sending an SMS in the background.
3. Choose the bank account to link.
4. Set a UPI PIN if none exists. The app usually asks for the last 6 digits of the debit card and its expiry date, then an OTP from the bank by SMS. These are entered **only on the official PhonePe screens**, never told to anyone.
Caution: the learner must never share the UPI PIN with anyone, including family members who are "helping" while they stand next to them.

## Task: Pay a shop by scanning its QR code
1. Open PhonePe and tap the button that says **Scan** (or scan icon) on the home screen. [CHECK]
2. Point the camera at the shop's QR code. Allow camera permission if asked.
3. Check that the **shop's name** shown matches the shop you are standing in.
4. Type the **amount** and double-check it. Tap the button to pay (usually **Pay** or **Proceed**). [CHECK]
5. Choose the bank account if asked, then enter the **UPI PIN** on the PIN screen.
6. A green tick or "Payment successful" screen appears. Show it to the shopkeeper if needed. Many shops also have a speaker that announces the payment.
Common worries: pressing the wrong amount (check before PIN, you can still go back), poor network (wait, do not pay again until you check history).

## Task: Send money to a person
Options on PhonePe: send to a **mobile number** (that person must be on UPI), to a **UPI ID**, or to a **bank account** number with IFSC. [CHECK: option names]
1. Open PhonePe and choose the option to send money to a mobile number or contact.
2. Select the person. **Check the name that appears.** If the name looks unfamiliar, stop.
3. Enter the amount, an optional short note, and tap to pay.
4. Enter the UPI PIN. Wait for the success screen.
Tip: for a first payment to someone new, send a small amount first and ask them to confirm.

## Task: Check bank balance
The app has an option to check the balance of the linked bank account (it asks for the UPI PIN because it is your private bank information). Entering the PIN to **check balance** is normal. [CHECK: location of the option]

## Receiving money (important)
- To receive money, the sender just needs your mobile number, UPI ID or QR code. **You do nothing and you never enter your PIN.**
- A **collect request** or "request money" message from someone asks **you to pay them**. Only approve it if you know the person and the reason. Approving needs your PIN because money leaves your account.

## When something goes wrong
- **Payment pending or failed but money deducted from the bank:** Do not pay again straight away. Check History for the final status. In most cases money that was deducted for a failed payment returns to the account automatically within a few working days. If it does not, use the Help option inside PhonePe, and also contact the bank. Do not state an exact number of days as a promise.
- **Paid the wrong person or wrong amount:** Money sent by UPI cannot be pulled back by the learner. Contact the person (if known) and ask for it back, report the transaction through **Help** in PhonePe, and inform the bank soon. Do not promise a result.
- **Payment limit message:** Banks and UPI have daily limits. The app or bank shows the actual limit.
- **App not working:** check internet, close and reopen the app, update the app from the official store.
- **Phone lost or stolen:** contact the mobile operator to block the SIM and the bank to block UPI. Then set up PhonePe again on a new phone.

## PhonePe-specific scams (teach these)
- "Enter your UPI PIN to **receive** money" is always a scam.
- Someone sends a QR code or a link on WhatsApp and says "scan to receive your money/prize/refund": scam.
- A caller says they are from PhonePe, a bank, or a "KYC team" and asks for OTP, PIN, or asks to install AnyDesk or similar: scam. Hang up.
- Fake PhonePe helpline numbers appear in web search results and social media replies. Use only **Help** inside the app.
- Fake "cashback" or "reward" links.
- A buyer on a classifieds site who says "I will pay you, scan this QR": scam.
