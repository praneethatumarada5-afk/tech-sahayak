## What it is
Rapido is a ride-booking app. You choose how to travel (a **bike taxi**, an **auto**, or a **cab**), the app finds a nearby driver, called a **captain**, and takes you from one place to another. Services depend on the city.

## Words explained simply
- **Pickup**: the place where the captain meets you.
- **Drop**: the place where you want to go.
- **Captain**: the driver of the bike, auto or cab.
- **Fare**: the price of the ride. The app shows an estimate before booking and the final amount at the end.
- **Ride OTP / PIN**: a short code shown **inside your own Rapido app** for each ride. You tell it to the captain **face to face** when you meet, so they can start the ride. This is the one case where telling a code is correct. [CHECK: whether the app shows a fresh OTP per ride or a fixed PIN in the current version]
- **Wallet**: money stored inside the Rapido app that can be used for rides.

## Task: Book a ride
1. Open Rapido. Allow location permission when asked, so the app knows where you are.
2. Check that the **pickup** point is correct. Move it if needed. Enter the **drop** location by typing the place name.
3. Choose **Bike**, **Auto** or **Cab**. Look at the estimated fare and waiting time. For a first ride, an auto or cab may feel more comfortable than a bike.
4. Choose how to pay (cash, UPI, or wallet), then confirm the booking. [CHECK: payment options in the learner's city]
5. Wait while the app finds a captain. You will see their **name, photo, vehicle number** and a way to call or message them.

## Before getting in (safety)
1. Match the **vehicle number plate** with the number shown in the app.
2. Check the captain's **name and photo**.
3. Ask "What is my name?" or let the captain confirm the drop location. Do not just say "Are you from Rapido?".
4. Tell the **ride OTP** from your own app to the captain **only after** these checks and only in person.
5. For bike rides, wear the **helmet** provided. [CHECK: helmet policy]
6. Share the ride details with a family member using the in-app option to share the trip. [CHECK: share feature name]. In an emergency call **112**.

## During and after the ride
- Follow the route on the app map to see the ride is going toward the drop.
- At the end, the app shows the **final fare**. Pay **the amount shown in the app** by the method you chose. If the captain asks for more than the app amount, do not argue; politely decline and report it from **Help** in the app. Small tips are optional.
- Rate the captain. Keep the receipt in your ride history if you need it later.

## Cancelling and waiting
- You can cancel before the ride starts. If a captain is already on the way, a **cancellation fee may apply**. The app shows it. [CHECK]
- Captain not arriving or asking you to cancel: wait a little, or cancel and book again. Do not pay anyone outside the app to "confirm" a ride.

## Common problems
- **No captains available:** try a different ride type, move the pickup point slightly, or wait a few minutes.
- **Wrong pickup pin:** the captain may go to the wrong spot. Call them through the app and describe a landmark.
- **Charged more than the estimate:** check the final fare breakdown (waiting, tolls, route change). If it still looks wrong, use **Help** on that ride.
- **Left something in the vehicle:** use **Help** on that ride to contact the captain through the app.
- **Payment issue:** do not pay twice. Check ride history, then use Help.

## Rapido-specific scams and risks (teach these)
- A "Rapido executive" calling to ask for OTP, PIN, card details or to install a screen-sharing app: scam.
- Captain asking you to cancel and pay him in cash or on a personal UPI number to avoid commission: avoid. Stay inside the app.
- Fake "support" numbers from search results. Use only **Help** in the app.
- A "booking fee" asked from you to start a ride or to claim "free ride coins": scam.
