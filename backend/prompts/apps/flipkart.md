## What it is
Flipkart is an Indian shopping app and website where you buy things (mobiles, clothes, home items, groceries, books and more), pay online or on delivery, and get them delivered to your address.

## Words explained simply
- **Cart**: a shopping basket where you collect items before buying.
- **Order**: your confirmed purchase. Each order has an **order ID** (a reference number).
- **Seller**: the shop that actually sells the item through Flipkart. The product page shows the seller name.
- **COD (Cash on Delivery)**: paying in cash or by UPI to the delivery person when the parcel arrives. Not available for every item or address.
- **Return window**: the number of days within which you can return an item. It is **shown on each product page** and differs by product. Some items are not returnable.
- **Refund**: money coming back to you after a return or cancellation.
- **EMI / Pay Later**: paying later or in instalments. Teach with care: it is a loan or credit, may have charges, and the learner should only use it if they understand the terms.

## Task: Find and buy something
1. Open Flipkart. Use the **search bar** at the top and type what you want (for example "steel water bottle"). [CHECK]
2. Open a product. Look at the **price**, **ratings**, **seller**, **delivery date** and the **return policy** on the product page.
3. Tap **Add to cart** or **Buy now**. [CHECK: label]
4. Check the **delivery address** carefully (name, house number, pincode, phone number). Add or change it if needed.
5. Choose how to pay: UPI, card, net banking, or Cash on Delivery if shown. Check the **total amount** (price + delivery fee, if any).
6. Place the order. You get an order confirmation and a message.
Teach the habit: compare two or three items, read some low-star reviews, and check the return policy before paying.

## Task: Track an order
Open **Orders** (usually found under the account or profile section of the app) and tap the order to see its status (confirmed, shipped, out for delivery, delivered). [CHECK: exact menu path] Delivery dates are estimates.

## Task: Cancel an order
Before the order is shipped, an order can usually be cancelled from the **Orders** section by opening the order and choosing the cancel option. After it is shipped, cancellation may not be possible and a return after delivery may be the option instead. [CHECK]

## Task: Return or replace an item
1. Open **Orders**, choose the delivered order and look for a return or replace option. It is available only inside the return window of that product. [CHECK]
2. Choose the reason and (if asked) upload photos.
3. Choose a pickup slot or follow the instructions shown.
4. Keep the item, box, tags and invoice safe until it is picked up.
Refunds go back to the original payment method, or sometimes to a Flipkart gift card or credit if the learner chooses it. The app shows the expected timeline. Do not promise a number of days.

## Delivery tips
- For some orders you receive a **delivery code (OTP)** by SMS or in the app. Give it to the delivery person **only at your door when you are taking the parcel**. Never tell it to a caller on the phone.
- Check the parcel is sealed and the right item before accepting. For some items, "open box delivery" may be offered. [CHECK] Take a short video while opening if there is a concern.
- If a parcel is damaged or wrong, do not throw it. Report it from **Orders** and **Help** immediately.

## Common mistakes and how to avoid them
- Wrong address or old phone number saved. Always re-check before placing the order.
- Paying outside the app for a "special discount". Never.
- Choosing Pay Later or EMI without understanding the charges.
- Ordering from an unknown seller with very few ratings and a price that is too good to be true.

## Flipkart-specific scams (teach these)
- "You won a prize or Flipkart lottery" calls, SMS or WhatsApp messages: scam. Flipkart does not ask for money to give you a prize.
- Fake "refund pending" or "order problem" calls asking for OTP, card details, or asking you to install a screen-sharing app: scam.
- Fake customer care numbers that appear in a search. Use **Help** inside the app only.
- Messages with "Big Billion Days" or festival sale links from unknown numbers: do not tap. Open the app directly instead.
- Someone asking you to pay by "scanning a QR to receive" a refund: scam. Refunds arrive on their own to your original payment method.
- Delivery person saying "tell me the OTP on the phone, I am outside": do not read an OTP over a call. Go to the door.
